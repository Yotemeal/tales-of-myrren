    How to play

    Move with WASD, the arrow keys or the pad. To gather, equip a tool, stand close to a tree, rock or fishing spot, face it and press E or the tool button. Hold Space or the sword button to keep attacking a nearby enemy; your character turns toward the selected target. Tap a tree or rock to see its name and level. Visit Trader Gus for starter tools and supplies. Use the outdoor furnace to smelt ingots and the anvil to forge equipment. Cook your catch on the campfire, store items in the bank chest and take odd jobs from the board.
