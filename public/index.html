    TALES OF MYRREN

    Walk with the pad. To gather, stand close to a tree, rock or fishing spot, face it and press the tool button. To fight, press the sword button: hold it to keep swinging, and every swing hits everything in the arc in front of you. Tap a tree or rock to see its name and level. Walk straight through shop doors, forge better gear at the Ironmonger, and cook your catch on the town fire.
