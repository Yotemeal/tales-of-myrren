# Woodland starter region (levels 1–30)

The starter map stays 1600×1600 with existing collision limits, shop interactions and saved coordinates. Five essential shops, well, job board, lamps, resources and combat remain.

## Implemented first stage
A three-layer perimeter of native pixel-art trees encloses the map visually. The existing impassable world-edge collision remains unchanged. Forest trees are decorative, depth-sorted and viewport-culled; they are not harvestable nodes.

## Next stages
- Create a compact timber village clearing and winding paths, preserving shop interaction points.
- Resize and detail timber buildings to match the native V31 character.
- Add ferns, roots, moss, fallen logs and varied woodland undergrowth.
- Create readable routes to gathering and combat clearings.
- Add a controlled exit to a future higher-level zone only after level-gating and destination exist.

Armour and equipment logic are out of scope while Work handles them.
